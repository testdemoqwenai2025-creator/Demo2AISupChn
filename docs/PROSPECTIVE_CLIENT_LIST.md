## Research Sources & Methodology

### Primary Sources
1. **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** - Title/role verification, company size
2. **Crunchbase** - Funding stage, growth metrics, recent news
3. **Company Websites** - Leadership pages, press releases, investor relations
4. **Industry Reports** - Gartner Magic Quadrants, Forrester Waves
5. **News Outlets** - TechCrunch, WSJ, Bloomberg, industry pubs

### Secondary Sources
1. **Conference Speaker Lists** - Supply Chain World, Gartner Symposium
2. **Podcast Appearances** - Supply Chain Insights, Talking Logistics
3. **Patent Filings** - Innovation indicators
4. **Job Postings** - Hiring trends indicate priorities
5. **Earnings Calls** - Executive commentary on challenges/opportunities
